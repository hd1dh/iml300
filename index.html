Projects

Project 1
Interactive Audio & Visual with p5.js

This is an interactive mini-game, inspired by a childhood game called Hot and Cold. A bee is hidden in this flower bed. When your mouse gets closer to the bee, the buzz sound of the bee will be louder. You need to click to find the bee according to the sound prompt.

Project 2
HyperNarrative

This project is based on a movie All About Lily Chou-Chou. I incorporated my feelings and thoughts into this website. It combines the forms of video, forums and dialogues to tell the several moments in the original story where Philia shares his spiritual world(Ether) with others through Lily's songs.

Project 3
Networked Care

By reimagining the traditional Chinese Almanac in a modern, accessible format, this project aims to preserve and document those intangible cultural elements. It shows a distinction between what is suitable to do and what is not to do every day, and the interaction between the calendar and living habit throughout the year.

Vibe Coding Exercises

Vibe Coding Exercise 1

This exercise was inspired by the exhibition work "Can Poetry Be Written By Machines?" by artist Sasha Stiles. In this work, I want to create a web allowing users to reflect on how the interaction between people and poetry affects the meaning of poetry itself.

Vibe Coding Exercise 2

This exercise was inspired by the reading article "What Screens Want", which mentioned that early Internet aesthetics tended to simulate the texture and form in reality, like notes, calendar, etc. I created a collective workbench.

Vibe Coding Exercise 3

This exercise is inspired by Laurel Schwulst. I created this UI based on her ideas about reducing excessive information on web pages and arranging websites in the form of a bookshelf.

Vibe Coding Exercise 4

The inspiration for this comes from Lauren McCarthy, who explored the impact of continuous surveillance on an individual's privacy and behavior.

Vibe Coding Exercise 5

This exercise is inspired by works by Chia Amisola in Bomb Magazine. They use the constantly emerging new pages as their artistic style.

Vibe Coding Exercise 6

This vibe coding was inspired by the article by Brandon Gregory, who mentioned the symptoms of inattention and how website design can assist those people to a certain extent.

Vibe Coding Exercise 7

Inspired by 100 Anti-Theses in Cyberfeminism Index. I borrowed the anti-thesis and made them into a player animation.

Vibe Coding Exercise 8

This is inspired by DJ_Dave. I wish to make a digital DJ panel, which you could use multiple index to control the output of sound.

Vibe Coding Follow up Exercise

I adapted Exercise 5 and made some modifications using Claude at first, making the positions of the page pop up not overlap.
