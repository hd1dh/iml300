The Twenty-Four Solar Terms are based on the Earth’s annual orbit around the Sun. The Earth’s orbital path is divided into 360 degrees, with the Spring Equinox at 0 degrees; each subsequent 15-degree segment constitutes a solar term, resulting in a total of 24 solar terms. These terms are determined by the movement of the constellations, the measurement of the sun’s shadow using a gnomon, and the Sun’s ecliptic longitude.
