Chinese Almanac
Traditional Chinese almanacs include elements such as the lunar calendar, solar terms, and the sexagenary cycle, along with guidelines on auspicious and inauspicious activities, as well as rules regarding the movements of the constellations. They not only reflect changes in astronomy and meteorology but also embody folk customs and beliefs.
Aims
Reimagining the Huangli in a modern, accessible format, this project aims to preserve and document those intangible cultural elements. Show a distinction between what is suitable to do and what is not to do every day, and the interaction between the calendar and living habit throughout the year.
