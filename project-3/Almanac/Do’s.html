Explanation of Terms for Daily Auspicious and Inauspicious Activities
Ritual & Worship
Ancestral Rites (祭祀) Ceremonies held at ancestral halls or temples, including the worship of ancestors and deities.
Prayer for Blessings (祈福) Visiting a temple to offer incense, fulfill vows, or pray to deities for blessings; also includes Taoist ritual ceremonies.
Praying for Offspring (求嗣) Supplicating deities for descendants (children).
Consecration Ceremony (开光) The ritual of enshrining and consecrating a newly crafted statue of a Buddha or deity.
Sacred Sculpting & Painting (塑绘) The painting of murals or carving of deity statues and figures in temples.
Ritual Ceremony (齐醮) Setting up a Taoist ritual altar to pray for peace and blessings.
Pre-Ceremony Purification (斋醮) The fasting and purification ritual before a ceremony.
Ritual Bathing (沐浴) Cleansing the body before rituals.
Thanksgiving to Deities (酬神) Fulfilling vows and thanking deities.
Temple Construction (造庙) Building temples or shrines.
Kitchen God Worship (祀灶) Ritual of worshipping the Kitchen God.
Incense Burning (焚香) Burning incense sticks.
Moving the Sacred Flame (出火) Relocating a deity's spiritual position.
Marriage
Wedding Ceremony (嫁娶) The formal marriage ceremony.
Betrothal & Engagement Gifts (纳采) Formal engagement process.
Exchange of Birth Dates (问名) Families exchange birth data to approve marriage.
Groom Marrying into Bride's Family (纳婿) Groom moves into bride's household.
Bride's First Return Home (归宁) Newlyweds visit bride's parents.
Setting Up the Marriage Bed (安床) Preparing wedding bed.
Hanging the Bed Curtain (合帐) Installing bed curtains.
Coming-of-Age Ceremony (冠笄) Marks adulthood.
Engagement Ceremony (订盟) Formal engagement ritual.
Adoption (进人口) Accepting adopted child.
Cutting Wedding Garments (裁衣) Preparing wedding clothing.
Funerary Rites
Tomb Repair (修墓) Maintenance of graves.
Exhumation & Bone Washing (启攒) Cleansing remains.
Breaking Ground for Burial (破土) Preparing burial ground.
Burial Ceremony (安葬) Funeral service.
Erecting a Tombstone (立碑) Installing gravestone.
Donning Mourning Garments (成服) Wearing mourning clothes.
Removing Mourning Garments (除服) End of mourning period.
Construction
Moving into a New Home (入宅) Housewarming ceremony.
Breaking Ground (起基动土) Start of construction.
Ridge Beam Installation (上梁) Installing roof beam.
Demolition (拆卸) Tearing down buildings.
Commerce & Business
Grand Opening (开市) Opening a business.
Signing Contracts (立券交易) Formal agreements.
Receiving Wealth (纳财) Collecting assets.
Seeking Wealth (求财) Pursuing profit.
Daily Life
Travel (出行) Journey and outings.
Moving Home (移徙) Relocation.
Planting (栽种) Growing crops.
Gathering (会友) Meeting friends.
Taking Office (赴任) Starting official duty.
